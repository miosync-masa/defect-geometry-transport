# Mode Selection and Collective Propagation: two regimes by which defect geometry controls interfacial electron transport

Toy-model study of how the *arrangement* of defects at an interface controls electron conductance, at fixed defect count.
Two-dimensional square-lattice tight-binding wire, Landauer–NEGF (Sancho–Rubio leads) and an exact on-site T-matrix.
Pure NumPy/SciPy; one script per experiment; every figure in the notes is regenerated by a script in `experiments/`.

**Core result.** Configurations with identical defect counts differ in conductance by up to 2×. The conductance decomposes exactly as
`G/G0 = (N_eff/N0) · D_eff` (effective transmitting channels × per-channel transparency). Two mechanisms, with different descriptors, control the outcome:

| mechanism | descriptor | where it dominates | key evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **mode selection** | transverse-mode-resolved defect overlap `A_mn` (esp. `A_fwd_diag = |Σ_j φ_n(y_j)²|²/v_n`) | few periods (≤4), generic arrangements | held-out R² 0.81 → 0.885; removes systematic under-prediction of periodic arrays |
| **collective propagation** | propagating-Bloch fraction of the effective unit cell (transfer matrix) | many periods (≳4–6) | finite-array dark bands = infinite-superlattice stop bands (r = 0.76); uncorrelated with free-electron Bragg (|r| ≤ 0.08); added value grows +0.02 → +0.18 at fixed N_def |

Real-space descriptors (counts, projections, nearest neighbours, g(r)) do not transfer to unseen periodic families; the free-electron Bragg condition does not locate the stop bands even at weak scattering.

## Method notes
* **Exact T-matrix.** For on-site defects, `T_dd = U (1 − G0_dd U)⁻¹` on the defect sites reproduces NEGF to 1e-15 and makes U-scans, energy sweeps and truncated multiple-scattering series (Born-1/2, independent-scatterer, pair) essentially free.
* **Decomposition.** With transmission eigenvalues `T_n`, `S1=ΣT_n`, `S2=ΣT_n²`: `N_eff=S1²/S2`, `D_eff=S2/S1`, so `G/G0 = (N_eff/N0)·D_eff` identically (participation ratio of the transmission spectrum).
* **Bloch descriptor.** Period from mode-weighted autocorrelation of defect x-positions; unit cell = actual defect pattern in one period; 2W×2W transfer matrix; fraction of open channels that remain propagating.

## Scope
2D single-band tight binding, on-site defects, elastic scattering, single E_F (plus Fermi-window averaging). No phonons, no dephasing, no real-Cu electronic structure. Statistical caveats per experiment are in the notes and in `docs/CLAIMS_PACKAGE_ATS.md`.
